PacMan

An ongoing Semester 3 software development project created for Night of the Nerds as part of my Bachelor's degree in Information & Communication Technology (ICT) at Fontys University of Applied Sciences. PacMan is a cooperative arcade game developed using Unity and C#, where two players share control of a single character. One player controls horizontal movement, while the other controls vertical movement. The project focuses on cooperative gameplay, software development, enemy AI, and UI/UX design.

Technologies

Unity C# Git MySQL

Highlights

  • Shared Two-Player Movement – Two players control a single character, with one handling horizontal movement and the other vertical movement.
  • Collectibles & Score Tracking – Players collect items throughout the maze to increase their score.
  • Countdown Timer & Game Over – A countdown timer controls the game duration, followed by a game-over screen displaying the final score.
  • Main Menu & Maze Layout – A functional main menu and a custom-designed maze with collision detection.
  • Enemy AI – Three enemy types with different movement behaviors: a BFS Hunter that uses pathfinding to navigate toward the player, a Patroller that follows a predefined route, and an Accelerating Chaser that temporarily increases its speed while pursuing the player.
  • UI/UX Design – Designing and improving the game's interface, including the main menu, score display, countdown timer, game-over screen, and visual feedback to support intuitive cooperative gameplay.
